Ingredients

  • 1 onion
  • 2 bell peppers, red and yellow, or any color
  • 1 pound lean meat, beef preferred, turkey, chicken, or vegan meat work too
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 16oz tub low fat cottage cheese
  • shredded cheddar cheese
  • (optional) 2 slices american cheese
  • (optional) additional garlic powder, chili powder, salt

Instructions

  1. Dice onion, cook it down
  2. Add meat, cook it down
  3. Add diced bell peppers, cook it down
  4. Add taco seasoning, cook a bit
  5. Add tub of cottage cheese, mix well until chunks dispersed
  6. top with cheddar cheese, serve with chips or tortillas
