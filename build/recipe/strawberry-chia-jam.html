Strawberry Chia Jam 🍓Dessert
Share Recipe 📧
https://youtube.com/shorts/yVAc-L6WlZc?si=ATvrkUtG2uK1oe63 🔗

Ingredients

  • 1 bag frozen strawberries (or other fruit)
  • 2 tbsp chia seeds
  • lemon juice
  • sweetener (sucralose or honey or maple syrup or sugar

Instructions

  1. In a saute pan, cook down straberries, add lemon juice and chia seeds, mash gently
  2. Let it cool
  3. Blend with sweetener
