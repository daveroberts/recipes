Ingredients

  • 2 pie crusts
  • 2 blocks reduced fat cream cheese
  • 1 32oz tub non fat light vanilla greek yogurt (Add vanilla if plain)
  • egg whites (about 4 eggs worth), or use real eggs
  • 2 cups sugar substitute
  • 1/2 cup flour
  • 1/2 tsp salt

Instructions

  1. Mix, put into pie crusts
  2. Bake at 325F for 65 minutes
  3. Remove and let it cool
  4. Place in fridge
